Ask: Jumper + finishing / elevation

This is a functional, competitive jumper for his age group with clear upside. He elevates well, gets a high release, and is willing to shoot under pressure. The foundation is not broken.

Current level: Above-average youth jumper with athleticism supporting the shot.

Work:

  • Widen and stabilize the base — especially contested.
  • Slow the load. Rushed gathers are where the elbow gets sloppy.
  • Hold the follow-through. Wrist snap plus a frozen finish.

Bottom line: Elevation and willingness to shoot are real advantages. The work is making the good form show up when the defense is close.
